# Performance Skill Application Notes

## How to call it

Say "use the Performance Skill" and provide a script, dialogue, character conflict, rough prompt or a description of a reference performance. You can name Kling, Seedance 2.0 or MiniMax H3; when no model is named, it outputs a generic master plus the minimal model differences.

## Minimum input

- Characters and their relationship
- The current scene and total duration
- The event or dialogue that happens
- The emotional result you want

When non-critical items are missing, the skill carries on with conservative assumptions that are easy to roll back; it only asks when something would change the facts of the story or create a real conflict.

## What the output focuses on

- Emotional cause and effect, and how it builds
- Actor behaviour that can be seen, heard and executed second by second
- Ownership of dialogue, pauses, breath and off-screen voices
- Camera position, and whether it moves, in service of the emotion
- A small amount of necessary continuity for identity, background, props and light
